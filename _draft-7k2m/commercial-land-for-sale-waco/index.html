Commercial Land for Sale in Waco

Land is the largest part of our current inventory: pad sites in front of shopping centers, retail and office tracts along Hewitt Drive and Highway 84, industrial acreage near Highway 6 and Loop 340, and I-35 frontage from Lorena to Bellmead.

Our land listings are below. On land, the questions are utilities, zoning, frontage, drainage and what the city will let you build, so every listing notes what we know and we will tell you what we don't.

We also represent buyers looking for a site, including users coming to Waco for the first time. Heart of Texas Goodwill's 5.5 acres on Hewitt Drive is a recent example. If you have a use and a footprint in mind, we can map the options.
