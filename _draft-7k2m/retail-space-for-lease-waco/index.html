Retail Space for Lease in Waco

Retail in Waco follows traffic. Valley Mills Drive and the Richland Mall area, Hewitt Drive through Woodway and Hewitt, Franklin Avenue, and the I-35 frontage from downtown to the Baylor side all draw different customers and different rents. Downtown and the Magnolia district bring visitors; the suburban corridors bring the daily trade.

Our current retail listings are below: inline suites, end caps, pad sites and freestanding buildings. Rates are quoted per square foot per year and usually exclude taxes, insurance and common area costs, so ask for the full occupancy number before comparing two spaces.
